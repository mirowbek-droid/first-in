Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-02. Describes version 2.0.

What the extension reads

On LinkedIn job search pages it reads the job cards already on your screen (title line, location line, labels such as "Easy Apply" or "Viewed") and the applicant count of each job. To get a count it requests that job's page from linkedin.com - LinkedIn's public job page first, and your signed-in job page only when the public one gives no exact number.

What it stores

In your browser only (Chrome's extension storage):

  • the applicant count last read for each job, with the time it was read;
  • whether LinkedIn marked that job promoted or reposted;
  • your filter settings;
  • the times of its own recent requests, to stay under LinkedIn's rate limit.
  • the date the extension was first used (for the 7-day trial of the filters) and whether the filters are unlocked.

What it sends, and to whom

Nothing about your LinkedIn activity is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The job counts, your filters and your settings never leave your browser. There are no analytics and no advertising.

The extension contacts two sites:

  • linkedin.com, to read the job pages described above;
  • extensionpay.com, to check whether this installation has unlocked the paid filters. For that it sends a random key created when the extension is installed. The key is not linked to your LinkedIn account.

If you buy the unlock

Payment is handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and its payment processor, Stripe. They receive your email address and your payment details; the developer can see the email address and that a payment was made, never your card. The extension itself only learns whether you have paid. Your email is used to restore your purchase on another browser and for nothing else.

What it never does

It does not apply to jobs, save or dismiss them, send messages, or change anything in your LinkedIn account. It does not read your profile, your messages or your contacts.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
