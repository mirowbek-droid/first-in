Support

Something not working? Most problems have one of the causes below.

The counts or the box do not appear

  • Refresh the LinkedIn tab. Chrome only loads a new or updated extension into a page when it reloads.
  • Check you are on a job search results page (the address contains /jobs/search).
  • If a red notice says LinkedIn changed its page, the extension needs an update. Updates install by themselves, usually within a day.

A badge shows <25 instead of a number

LinkedIn did not give an exact count for that job yet. Open More in the box and press Check, or open the job.

The box says "pacing the reads"

LinkedIn allows about 20 job pages a minute per account. First In waits rather than go over, so the last counts on a busy page can take up to a minute.

The filters are greyed out

Your LinkedIn is in a language First In does not read yet. It reads English, French, Spanish and German. Applicant counts work in every language.

Promoted or Reposted says "Not known yet"

LinkedIn only shows these on the job's own page. A job moves out of "Not known yet" once you open it.

Still stuck

Write to mirow.bek@gmail.com with the address of the LinkedIn page and what you see. A screenshot helps.
